ABOUT / ALEXANDER IBARRA

Making Art that Inspires others to Create

California born DMA artist Alexander Ibarra creates because he is able to and hopes you will too

I'm a fledging artist who wishes to utilize my technological background, growing up in the silicon valley, to create art that sheds light on the digital plane and how to best represent it, art as an expressive release with which anyone can achieve, and the realms beyond reality and how we might perceive them. My practice flourishes in the obscure and the abstract with opaque works that allow you to wonder and wander.

Narrative based works include topics of digital ownership rights and citizen privacy within an ever growing surveillance state. As a person who has been exposed to most forms of digital storage I am appalled by the lack of agency the consumer has had in the face of our media and hope to correct this wrong through exposure through the very digital media that may be harder to obtain in the future. I utilize primarily digital media but am well versed in other forms of artistic practice such as sketch art, photography, and videography

I grew up with the internet during a time most could only dream to go back to, I ought to use that connection to the best of my ability. Twisting reality as seen within photographs or interpreting them with obscure sketch drawings is my preferred method of expression yet am always learning new ways of translating the vision within my mind to reality.
